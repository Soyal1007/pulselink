### **Executive Summary**

> **PulseLink** is a zero-delay pre-hospital emergency care platform designed to bridge the operational and diagnostic data gap between field paramedics and hospital emergency departments. By delivering real-time vitals sync, AI-assisted ECG arrhythmia detection, telemetry broadcasting, and multi-hospital bed/trauma capacity management, PulseLink optimizes patient routing and guarantees clinical readiness before the ambulance arrives.

---

### **Core Platform Modules**

1. **Paramedic Field Suit & Live Telemetry Unit**
   - Real-time Vitals Streaming (SpO2, HR, NIBP, Temp)
   - On-device Defibrillator & ECG Signal Processing
   - One-touch Emergency Redirection & Hospital Broadcast
2. **Clinical Smart Chat & ER Command Center**
   - Bi-directional Directive & Audio Sync
   - Automated Triage Scoring & AI Warning Flags
3. **Multi-Hospital Fleet & Capacity Coordinator**
   - Dynamic ICU / Trauma Bed Availability Matrix
   - Zero-Delay Routing & Hospital Preparation
